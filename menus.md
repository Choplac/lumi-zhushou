# Menu map (tour 11-14)

English | [简体中文](menus.zh-CN.md)

Full-res tap coordinates (720x1280). "Auto" = the bot's `freebies`/`claim` step does it.

## Free things
- **Shop 每日赠礼**: 20 stamina a day. Open the shop (44,184), then tap 每日赠礼 (84,206). Auto.
- **Season pass (S1赛季 48,290 → 通行证 150,920)**:
  - An intro carousel may show first; its X opens the pass.
  - 旅行目标 tab (530,1150): 4 daily tasks at 20 pass points each. Auto.
  - Free-track levels: the left column of 露米娅之旅 (180,1150), marked by red dots. Auto.
  - One level costs 50 points.
- **特惠礼包**: adventure screen, 3rd left icon (44,370).
  - It is a chain: free 10 stamina → free 2,000 coins → ¥6 (stop there).
  - The bot claims only cells with a 免费 tag. Auto.
- **Daily tasks** (menu 100,1200 → 任务 416,980 → 每日任务): the 15–75 point chests. Auto (`claim`).
- **Guild chat red envelopes (瑞兽送福)**:
  - Claiming one gave 38 stamina and visits that player's home; the X at the bottom returns.
  - Manual: their positions in the chat change.
- **新图探秘 event** (4天 icon on the adventure screen's right side): 挑战 tab ladder tasks.
  - One 领取 tap claims all of them (190 points, then milestone rewards).
  - Manual: event icons come and go.
- **寻宝总动员** (活动集合 → chest): map pieces drop on adventure runs.
  - 碎片求助 asks the guild for pieces (free).
  - The full map pays 88,888 coins + 666–2,233 stamina.

  - Big rewards; about 21 hours left on 11-14.

- **归星 其他挑战 trades**: give 噜咪 for items (for example 5 purple → 万能碎片).
- **露米娅联盟 5th boss** (对战 → 道馆战): lost on auto.
  - The boss is level 150 with a 95-score 噜咪.
